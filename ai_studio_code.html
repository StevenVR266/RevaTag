1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the VR application Reva Tag, developed and published by StevenGames ("we", "us", or "our") for Meta Horizon / Quest devices. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data processed through our application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to ensure multiplayer functionality, player safety, and game performance:

  • Meta User ID / Oculus ID: A unique string identifier assigned by the Meta platform used to authenticate your account, load your profile, and maintain gameplay progress.
  • IP Address: Stored and processed via our backend services (e.g., Microsoft PlayFab / Photon) for networking, matchmaking, and player moderation (such as temporary or permanent IP bans for rule violations).
  • Game & Technical Data: In-game statistics, cosmetic inventory, and basic device performance metrics needed to maintain server stability.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

We process this data exclusively for:

  • Providing online multiplayer and matchmaking features.
  • Saving your progress and in-game items.
  • Moderation and community protection against toxicity or cheating.
  • Fixing bugs and maintaining server reliability.

4. Third-Party Services

Reva Tag utilizes trusted third-party providers. We encourage you to review their privacy policies:

  • Meta Horizon: Meta Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft Azure PlayFab: Microsoft Privacy Statement
  • Photon Engine (Exit Games): Photon Privacy Policy

You have the right to request permanent deletion of all data associated with your account. To submit a request, please send an email to:

6. Children’s Privacy

Reva Tag is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum legal age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at tyron12@tw4wg.onmicrosoft.com so we can remove the data immediately.
